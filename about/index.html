Who are we?

And why i started Ametrine

Our Founder Noticxs

I started Ametrine, as a project to save the internet. And by save the internet I mean removing the control that big corporate companies have on our software.

I believe that software should be free, open, and accessible to everyone. Not just the people who can afford it or the people who work for big companies.

At Ametrine, we are committed to building software that respects your freedom and privacy. We believe that everyone should have access to software that empowers them, without compromising their rights.

— Noticxs, CEO & Founder of Ametrine Foundation
