Who are we?

🙋‍♀️ A short introduction - Ametrine Foundation is a software company who strives to make open-source projects that are free for all customers, whislst maintaining a high quality standard. Here at Ametrine Foundation we only currently have 2 people helping to make the world of free open-source software a better place: our founder Noticxs and our web developer Phyzzicc.
🍪 Fun fact - The original Ametrine Project was made on a Dell Latitude e7250 later updated to a Dell Latitude 5300 which is what most projects are made on even till this day.

Our values

Here at Ametrine, we believe your data is yours and we refuse to ever collect any data from you. We also believe in the idea that all software should be accessible to everybody and no features should be locked behind a paywall. We will never, release any software locked behind a paywall and all our projects will be available to everyone equally. We believe that everyone should have access to software that empowers them, without compromising their rights. At Ametrine, we are committed to building software that respects your freedom and privacy.
